# Recruitment verification policy

## Source priority

1. Employer career site or official application system.
2. Employer recruiting account or official announcement.
3. University employment center reproducing an employer announcement.
4. Reputable recruitment platform only where allowed by the user's scope.

For campus records, prefer a direct official application URL. For eligible executive-assistant or secretary social roles, BOSS直聘 and 猎聘 links are acceptable when the listing is current and the employer scale is verifiable.

## Inclusion checks

Confirm all of the following:

- employer has at least 500 employees;
- application is currently open;
- role is full-time and relevant to at least one target category;
- graduation-year eligibility is compatible with the user for campus programs;
- direct link resolves to the employer or specific platform listing;
- opening/deadline claims are supported by a current source;
- employer nature and funding/listing status are labeled accurately.

## Exclusions

Exclude:

- internship-only or campus-ambassador-only openings;
- closed or expired programs without a live replacement;
- pages that announce recruitment but provide no verifiable open application route;
- irrelevant role families;
- duplicate records;
- social roles outside the user's explicit social-recruitment categories;
- social executive-assistant/secretary roles requiring more than three years of experience or clearly senior executive credentials.

## Matching

Base the score on explicit resume evidence: relevant responsibilities, quantified outcomes, industry exposure, tools, languages, certifications, leadership, and location fit. Do not invent experience. Explain the strongest two or three transferable signals in one sentence.

## Daily report

Report total active employers and counts for added, updated, and removed records. Name material changes and provide direct links. If nothing changed, state that explicitly.
